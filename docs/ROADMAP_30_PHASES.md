# JPAI — 31-Phase Development Roadmap

## Stage I — Foundations & Intelligence Core
1. Core Architecture — repository, modular structure, local runtime, API/UI shells, configuration, diagnostics and tests.
2. Local Intelligence Infrastructure — local model runtime, discovery, inference interfaces, capability profiles and offline operation.
3. Universal API Gateway — local governed ingress, capability allowlisting, authentication, rate limits, payload bounds and telemetry.
4. Multi-Agent Orchestration — specialist task lifecycle, council, human approvals, audit events and performance telemetry.
5. Memory & Knowledge Infrastructure — typed local memory, retrieval and knowledge graph.
6. Professional Intelligence Registry — specialist profiles, capability matrices, dynamic registration, explainable matching and scorecards.
7. Quality Governance & Continuous Improvement — evidence-gated Build → Reassess → Improve → Test → Verify cycles and sequential root-cause issue resolution.

## Stage II — Leadership & Professional Workforce
8. Executive Council & Leadership.
9. Multi-Agent Workforce Engine — advanced task decomposition, delegation, queue workers and resilient scheduling.
10. Research & Intelligence Directorate — Web Intelligence crawler, bounded scraper, provenance-aware extraction, schema.org JSON-LD metadata capture, local PDF/DOCX/TXT/Markdown/CSV extraction, bounded document indexing and search, persistent scheduled monitoring, page-version history, content-change alerts, local research index and specialist-agent ingestion, unified evidence search across web and uploaded sources, researcher-authored claim records, verbatim citation validation, support/contradiction/context links and transparent evidence-balance summaries.
11. Technology & Engineering Directorate — governed engineering project lifecycle, requirements/design/implementation/verification gates, test-artifact references, risk review and durable audit history.
12. Creative Writing & Editorial Intelligence — governed editorial briefs, outlines, drafts, source-aware fact-checking, editorial review, explicit publication approval and durable audit history.

## Stage III — Creative, Business & Enterprise
13. Graphic Design & Visual Intelligence — persistent design briefs, design specifications, asset manifests, accessibility and review/approval gates.
14. Video, Audio & Animation Studio — governed scripts, storyboards, production plans, production evidence, rights metadata and final review.
15. Creative Development & Digital Experiences — product discovery, requirements, UX specifications, implementation artifacts, QA and launch review.
16. Business, Marketing & Customer Intelligence — customer research, strategy, campaign assets, activation approval, measurement and optimization.

## Stage IV — Financial, Trading & Specialised Intelligence
17. Financial Intelligence & ETI Integration — financial provenance, data mapping, analysis assumptions, risk review, reporting and human approval; scoped ETI adapter remains separate.
18. T-Bots Universal Trading Intelligence — universal market scope, data quality, strategy research, backtest/paper evidence, risk limits and deployment-eligibility governance; no live order execution.
19. Business & Enterprise Automation, including ELSIM — process maps, workflow design, permissions, simulation, owner approval and scoped action allowlists; execution adapters remain separate.
20. Media & Journalism Intelligence, including X-STUDIOS — assignment, sourced research, drafts, fact-check, editorial review, corrections and publication approval.
21. Personal Intelligence & JPTI Integration.

## Stage V — Advanced Autonomy & Intelligence
22. Advanced Reasoning & Decision Intelligence.
23. Autonomous Project Execution.
24. Adaptive Learning & Agent Evolution.
25. Advanced Automation & Tool Intelligence.
26. Autonomous Software Development.

## Stage VI — Interface, Security & World-Class Optimisation
27. World-Class JPAI Command Centre.
28. Desktop & Android Ecosystem.
29. Enterprise Security & Governance.
30. Performance & Reliability Engineering.
31. Final Integration & Operational Certification.

Phases may be dependency-aware and partially parallelised where safe. A phase is not complete because its documentation exists: completion requires working implementation, tests, security review, failure/recovery checks and evidence against acceptance criteria. Every phase follows the continuous Build → Reassess → Improve → Test → Verify cycle and the mandatory assess → identify → determine root cause → fix → test → verify issue-resolution protocol.
