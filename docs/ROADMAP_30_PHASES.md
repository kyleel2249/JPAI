# JPAI — 30-Phase Development Roadmap

## Stage I — Foundations & Intelligence Core
1. Core Architecture — repository, modular structure, local runtime, API/UI shells, configuration, diagnostics and tests.
2. Local Intelligence Infrastructure — local model runtime, discovery, inference interfaces, capability profiles and offline operation.
3. Universal API Gateway — local governed ingress, capability allowlisting, authentication, rate limits, payload bounds and telemetry. Multi-provider credential vaulting remains future scope.
4. Multi-Agent Orchestration — specialist task lifecycle, council, human approvals, audit events and performance telemetry. Full dependency-aware autonomous planning remains future scope.
5. Memory & Knowledge Infrastructure — typed local memory, retrieval and knowledge graph. Embeddings, automatic extraction and contradiction resolution remain future scope.

## Stage II — Professional Workforce
6. Professional Intelligence Registry — specialist profiles, capability matrices, dynamic specialist registration, explainable capability/performance matching and scorecards.
7. Executive Council & Leadership.
8. Multi-Agent Workforce Engine — advanced task decomposition, delegation, queue workers and resilient scheduling.
9. Research & Intelligence Directorate.
10. Technology & Engineering Directorate.

## Stage III — Creative, Business & Enterprise
11. Creative Writing & Editorial Intelligence.
12. Graphic Design & Visual Intelligence.
13. Video, Audio & Animation Studio.
14. Creative Development & Digital Experiences.
15. Business, Marketing & Customer Intelligence.

## Stage IV — Financial, Trading & Specialised Intelligence
16. Financial Intelligence & ETI Integration.
17. T-Bots Universal Trading Intelligence.
18. Business & Enterprise Automation, including ELSIM.
19. Media & Journalism Intelligence, including X-STUDIOS.
20. Personal Intelligence & JPTI Integration.

## Stage V — Advanced Autonomy & Intelligence
21. Advanced Reasoning & Decision Intelligence.
22. Autonomous Project Execution.
23. Adaptive Learning & Agent Evolution.
24. Advanced Automation & Tool Intelligence.
25. Autonomous Software Development.

## Stage VI — Interface, Security & World-Class Optimisation
26. World-Class JPAI Command Centre.
27. Desktop & Android Ecosystem.
28. Enterprise Security & Governance.
29. Performance & Reliability Engineering.
30. Final Integration & Operational Certification.

Phases may be dependency-aware and partially parallelised where safe. A phase is not complete because its documentation exists: completion requires working implementation, tests, security review, failure/recovery checks and evidence against acceptance criteria. Every phase follows the continuous Build → Reassess → Improve → Test → Verify cycle.
