# Professional Intelligence Registry — Design Baseline

The registry will model the organisation of JPAI's digital workforce without equating every job title with a separate model or agent.

## Entity distinctions
- **Role:** job definition, responsibilities, competencies and review criteria.
- **Agent:** configured worker instance assigned one or more compatible roles.
- **Model:** inference engine selected for a task.
- **Tool:** callable capability with a schema, permission scope and execution policy.
- **Department:** organisational grouping, leadership and domain ownership.

## Registry requirements for its implementation phase
- Searchable professional and role catalogue.
- Department hierarchy and reporting relationships.
- Competency, responsibility and output definitions.
- Agent creation, configuration, versioning and lifecycle status.
- Model/tool assignments and explicit permission scopes.
- Team composition, delegation, supervision and independent review.
- Memory boundaries, performance scorecards and change history.
- Conflict-of-interest and separation-of-duties controls for high-risk work.

## Examples of role families
Executive leadership; researchers and fact-checkers; software architects, developers, QA and security engineers; quantitative analysts, traders and independent risk reviewers; business strategists and project managers; writers, editors, designers, animators, producers and creative developers; journalists and marketers; privacy, compliance and audit specialists; knowledge curators and customer support professionals.

The detailed role catalogue will be expanded and versioned in the dedicated workforce phases. No role receives execution authority merely by being registered.
