# JPAI professional specialisation catalogue

Primary positioning:

**Software Engineer | Full-Stack Developer | AI & Intelligent Systems Developer | FinTech & Algorithmic Trading Systems Developer | Technology Entrepreneur**

The machine-readable catalogue is maintained in `backend/jpai_api/agents/professional_catalog.py` and exposed by `GET /api/v1/agents/catalog`.

It includes 11 categories and more than 150 role labels across core identity, software engineering, AI and automation, quantitative finance and FinTech, data science, cybersecurity, entrepreneurship and business, web and e-commerce, content and media, product/platform ecosystems, and technology strategy.

Role labels are used to configure specialist agent expertise and knowledge routing. They are not evidence of formal employment history, regulated professional status, academic qualification, licence or certification.

The default specialist workforce is a curated set of 16 agents. A single agent can cover multiple closely related role labels; the taxonomy remains broader than the seeded workforce so the registry can expand without changing the core architecture.
